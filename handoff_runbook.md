# Handoff Runbook

## Scope and accountable decision

- Who owns operation, monitoring, escalation, change approval, and fallback?
- Can another qualified person reproduce the end-to-end evidence without verbal help?

## Evidence

Link each material claim to a process step, record, rule, interface behavior,
test result, or stakeholder observation.

## Limits and revision trigger

State what current evidence does not establish and the condition that would
require this artifact or its conclusion to change.
